About Newwoodland

Where Comfort Meets Togetherness

Located in Bhadrapur-8, Jhapa, Nepal, Newwoodland Hotel & Lounge is a family-friendly hotel committed to providing comfortable accommodation and welcoming hospitality.

We offer a relaxed environment for families, friends and travelers, supported by attentive service, well-maintained surroundings and genuine Nepali hospitality. Our focus is to provide guests with a comfortable and pleasant stay from arrival to departure.
